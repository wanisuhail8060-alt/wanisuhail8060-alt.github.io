Delete Your Account

Last updated · 11 August 2026

The short version

  • You can delete your HabitFlow account and all synced data at any time.
  • The fastest way is right inside the app — one tap in Settings.
  • If you can't access the app, you can email us and we'll do it for you.

Delete from inside the app

  1. Open HabitFlow on your device.
  2. Go to Settings (bottom navigation).
  3. Scroll to Account and tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm the prompt. Your cloud data is wiped immediately and the app clears itself on this device.

Signed out already? Sign back in with the same Google account first, then follow the steps above.

Request deletion by email

If you can't open the app or would prefer we handle it, send us an email from the address tied to your HabitFlow account and we'll delete it manually within 7 days.

Please include:

  • The subject line: Delete my HabitFlow account
  • The Google email address you signed in with
  • Your HabitFlow username (if you remember it)

Email a deletion request

What gets deleted

When your account is deleted, the following are permanently removed from our cloud database and storage:

  • Your account profile — Google user ID, email, display name, username, time zone, avatar image.
  • All synced habits, check-ins, streak freezes, and weekly-challenge progress.
  • Sync bookkeeping and any push-notification token tied to your account.

Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone. If you want to keep a copy, use Settings → Save backup before deleting.

What stays on your device

Deleting your cloud account does not touch data stored on other people's devices. On the device where you delete, the app also clears the local database as part of the flow. If you want a fully clean slate, you can uninstall HabitFlow afterwards.

Data retention

  • Account & synced data: deleted immediately upon your request. No copies are retained.
  • Backups: any encrypted infrastructure backups roll off within 30 days.
  • Legal / abuse logs: we may retain minimal records only where required by law.
